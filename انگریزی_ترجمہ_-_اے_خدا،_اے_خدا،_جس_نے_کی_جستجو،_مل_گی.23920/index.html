Praise to the God​

Original Poetry in Urdu by Mr. Muzaffar Warsi​

Translated by: Hashim Syed Mohammad Bin Qasim
Performed by: Adnan Sami Khan (for movie SARGAM)​

Aye Khuda, Aye Khuda (Ye God, Ye God)​

Ye God, Ye God
The one who makes a quest for Thee
You are discovered by he
You are the guide for all​

This earth and the sky
And beyond them
In all the galaxies
There is your presence​

Ye God, Ye God
The one who makes a quest for Thee
You are discovered by he
You are the guide for all​

Each Dawn breaks
With a new color
The greens and flowers Sprout
From the Chest of Stones​

Ye God, Ye God
The one who makes a quest for Thee
You are discovered by he
You are the guide for all​

In every star
There is a universe of its own
The moon and the sun
Reflect your tone​

Ye God, Ye God
The one who makes a quest for Thee
You are discovered by he
You are the guide for all​
